Google OAuth setup required

Enable the Google Drive and Google Docs APIs, create a Chrome Extension OAuth client, and use this extension ID:

Then replace the placeholder oauth2.client_id in manifest.json with your OAuth client ID and reload the extension.

Privacy: translation runs locally in Chrome. Google authorization is used only when you choose to create the formatted copy in your own Google Drive.
The formatted-copy workflow preserves the copied document's tables, images, lists, headings, headers, footers, footnotes, and layout. Text with mixed inline formatting is translated in smaller runs so bold/italic/link styling can be retained; that can occasionally make a sentence translate less naturally than the plain-text preview.
